A platforming and combat game featuring boss battles and dynamic terrain.

Academic project · Unity (C#)

About the game

Flint & Steel is a 2.5D action platformer where you play as Scally Wagburn, who — together with his trusted companion Flint — sets out to find the world-renowned gold-plated ruby hook. Along the way Steel joins the adventure, and the crew faces off against Bucky Neerwater.


Built by a student team at Niagara College, the game mixes tight platforming with combat encounters, boss battles, and dynamic terrain.


My role

  • General programmer & level designer on the team
  • Developed a cross-input system supporting both keyboard/mouse and controller configurations
  • Implemented UI functionality for the main menu and pause systems
  • Designed and integrated audio systems, including custom sound effects

Development Story

Flint & Steel came together during the winter semester of our second year at Niagara College. Our team had four months to take a 2.5D action platformer from concept to a playable build.


What I'm proudest of is how much of the game we shipped. A full adventure with boss battles, dynamic terrain, and a story the team wrote together. Built by our team in a single semester.
